About CheapHub

CheapHub is a consumer savings publication operated by MarshMack Media LLC. We aim to help readers understand offers before they leave for a merchant.

What you will find

Buying guidance, selected comparisons, budget guides and, when approved and verified, curated deals and coupons.

Our approach

We explain price basis, material terms and why an offer may be useful. CheapHub.com and associated intellectual property are owned by DBMM Anderson LLC and licensed to MarshMack Media LLC.

Who CheapHub serves

CheapHub is designed for shoppers who want a practical answer before they buy: what the offer really costs, who qualifies, which restrictions matter and whether a lower-priced option fits the same need.

What CheapHub is not

CheapHub is not a merchant and does not control checkout, inventory or fulfillment. We do not describe a price as live or lowest unless an authoritative current source supports that claim.
