Bills guide

A practical subscription audit

Find overlapping plans and renewals you may have forgotten.

CheapHub Editorial · Updated September 2026 · Research guide

Use this guide to compare the complete purchase, not just the promotional headline. Work through the sections in order and keep the merchant’s final terms with your records.

List recurring charges

Review statements across cards and app stores for at least two billing cycles. Note annual renewals separately.

Rank by use

Keep plans that deliver ongoing value, downgrade plans with unused features and cancel duplicates.

Check cancellation terms

Save confirmation and the date service ends. Review whether a switch triggers early termination fees.

Decide what replaces what

Canceling one service only saves money if another purchase does not replace it. Identify free, library, employer or bundled alternatives first.

Schedule the next review

Put annual renewals and promotional end dates on a calendar. A fifteen-minute quarterly review can catch price changes and unused plans.

Our approach

This is general buying guidance based on editorial research. Merchant prices, terms and availability must be checked directly before purchase.
