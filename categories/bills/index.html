Bills and Subscriptions

How to shop this category

Make the total cost visible

Use the post-promotion price

Introductory rates can make a plan look inexpensive. Calculate the total for the initial term and the expected cost afterward.

List every recurring fee

Equipment, taxes, data limits, add-ons, service charges and required bundles can materially change the monthly total.

Review switching costs

Include activation, installation, device payoff, early termination fees and any service overlap during the change.

Keep confirmation records

Save the offer terms, order confirmation and cancellation number, then review the first two bills for differences.
