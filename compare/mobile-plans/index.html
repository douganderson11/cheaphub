Compare mobile plans

Look at coverage where you live and travel, data priority, fees, device terms and the post-promotion price.

CheapHub Editorial · Updated September 2026

Use the same household, dates, usage and term length for every option. Mark any number that is promotional, estimated or available only to selected customers.

Check coverage

Use provider maps as a starting point and confirm real-world experience where possible.

Read the data rules

Unlimited plans may differ on hotspot use and speed after thresholds.

Calculate switching cost

Include device payoff, activation and taxes.

Separate network from plan

Two brands may use related networks but offer different data priority, roaming, support and device policies.

Audit the first bills

Promotional credits may take time to appear. Review activation fees, trade-in credits and the regular monthly charge against the written order summary.
