Travel comparison

Direct booking or travel platform

Compare the final trip terms, support responsibility, loyalty benefits and cancellation rules.

CheapHub Editorial · Updated September 2026

Use the same household, dates, usage and term length for every option. Mark any number that is promotional, estimated or available only to selected customers.

Match the itinerary

Use identical dates, room or seat type, travelers and included extras.

Check support

Know whom to contact if a flight changes or a property cannot honor a booking.

Compare flexibility

A slightly higher direct rate may have different change or loyalty terms.

Compare payment timing

Some channels charge immediately, others at the property, and foreign-currency conversions can change the total.

Check loyalty tradeoffs

Third-party reservations may not earn points or receive member benefits. Value those benefits only if you are likely to use them.

Comparison limits

Rates and eligibility differ by person, place and date. This framework does not include live quotes or every provider. Confirm final terms directly.
