How we choose and check offers

An offer earns space because it has a clear consumer use, a documented source and terms we can explain.

Selection

Editors consider relevance, total cost, eligibility, restrictions, merchant destination and freshness. Compensation may exist but cannot replace basic verification.

Verification

Before publishing, we review the merchant terms and record when they were checked. We remove or mark expired offers and correct material errors.

Guides

Guides describe their criteria and limitations. Research from specifications is identified as research; we do not claim hands-on testing without it.

Source records

Each monetized offer should retain its merchant source, destination, price or discount basis, restrictions, verification timestamp, expiration information and accountable editor.

Corrections and expiration

Offers that cannot be reconfirmed are removed from active listings or labeled expired. Material editorial errors are corrected, and readers can report a problem from the Contact page.
