Home comfort upgrades under $100

Spend the $100 on one room job you already have. Amazon listings were price-checked on October 12, 2026, and the Target towel, sponge, wipe, and mop cards still carry a September 28 or September 29 check.

CheapHub Editorial · Updated October 12, 2026 · Selection framework

About this guide

These cards are published catalog listings with a merchant photo and a locked product page. Each card shows the merchant, the price recorded on its last-checked date, how that price was checked, any restrictions, and that date. A verified-discount badge appears only when the merchant page itself showed a typical or list price above the selling price. Every other card is a price check. The Amazon rows in this guide were checked on October 12, 2026. The Target rows were last checked on September 28 or September 29, 2026.

Spend the $100 on a job the room already has. If morning light is the problem, buy the curtains and stop there. If the hard floor is the weekly chore, the mop kit is the purchase, and a second sponge pack can wait.

Two Amazon pages showed a typical price

The badge is on for these two because Amazon showed a typical price above the selling price on October 12. The Furinno Simple Design side tables, set of 2 in French oak were $21.05 against a typical price of $44.64. The set is two tables. Each one measures 20 by 20 by 19.6 inches, in composite wood. The listing says the top holds 30 pounds and the lower shelf holds 20 pounds. Typical prices move, so read that comparison again at checkout. The SimpleHome cream blackout curtains, 63 inch, 2 panels were $9.49 against a typical price of $18.99. Each panel is 40 inches wide and 63 inches long, with six grommets at a 1.6-inch inner diameter. The face is cream and the backing is white. The listing says to wash them cold and skip the bleach. Measure the rod and the drop before you treat 63 inches as a fit.

Cloths, then two different sponges

The Amazon Basics microfiber cleaning cloths, 24-pack were $9.99 on October 12. The card is a price check. Each cloth is 16 by 12 inches. Wash the pack separately the first time, and confirm the cart is the 24-count. The card notes the price often sits around $9 to $11.

Target's two Scotch-Brite packs are separate products. Both were $3.49 on September 28, and both cards are price checks. The Scotch-Brite heavy-duty scrub sponges, 3-count are the abrasive pack for pots that can take a scrub. Coated nonstick belongs with the Scotch-Brite zero-scratch scrub sponges, 3-count. That card says to test a corner when the finish is unfamiliar.

Wipes on September 29, a mop kit the day before

The Clorox Fresh Scent disinfecting wipes, 75-count were $5.49 at Target on September 29. Target's product data that day showed $5.49, and the card is a price check. The card calls them bleach-free. Follow the contact time on the canister for a disinfection claim. The fresh scent is a fragrance, so a sensitive user can read the label before buying the 75-count. The Swiffer WetJet floor mop starter kit was $24.99 on September 28, also a price check. Pads and solution after the starter kit are a separate spend, and that refill cost can run past the price of the mop. Re-check the Target price at checkout.

A guest-bath towel set

The Room Essentials 6-piece Everyday Striped dorm towel set was $20.00 at Target on September 28. The card is a price check. It frames the set as a budget pick for a dorm or a guest bath, and it says to expect thinner fabric than a premium Threshold towel. Confirm the mix of bath towels, hand towels, and washcloths on the product page, since the card asks you to re-check the set contents.

Keep the ceiling honest

Tax and shipping still sit on top of these prices. A $24.99 mop kit can pick up refill cost after the first pads, and curtains plus the side tables is a second decision. The grid is the photographed listings filed as lighting, bath, cleaning, home decor, or furniture, with a checked price under $100. This week that is the tables, the curtains, the cloths, both sponge packs, the wipes, the mop kit, and the towel set. A further item joins the grid once this week's catalog has a locked photo and a merchant page for it under $100.
