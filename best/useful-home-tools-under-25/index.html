Useful home tools under $25

Eleven small tools for the jobs a house hands you every month, each under $25. Amazon listings in this guide were price-checked on November 23, 2026.

CheapHub Editorial · Updated November 23, 2026 · Selection framework

About this guide

These cards are published catalog listings with a merchant photo and a locked product page. Each card shows the merchant, the price recorded on its last-checked date, how that price was checked, any restrictions, and that date. A verified-discount badge appears only when the merchant page itself showed a typical price above the selling price. A list price on the page by itself leaves the card as a price check. All eleven Amazon rows here were checked on November 23, 2026. Two of them, the extension cord pair and the BLACK+DECKER pliers, carry the badge. The DEWALT bit set also shows a typical price above its selling price, and that gap is small enough that the card stays a price check. Amazon's typical price is the 90-day median customers paid in the Amazon store, excluding limited-time promotions.

Buy for the job that's already on your list. A tape measure and a decent driver handle most of what an apartment or a house asks for. Everything after those two is for a specific task you can name today.

Start with a tape

The Craftsman 25-foot tape measure, model CMHT37225S, was $12.88 on November 23. Amazon shows a list price of $14.98 on the page, and that list price is all it shows, so the card stays a price check. It has been a steady buy: every check since November 3 landed between $12.84 and $13.13. Twenty-five feet covers a room wall to wall, which is why we'd call it the first tool on this page.

The Klein Tools 16-foot compact tape measure was $12.98 against a list price of $13.98, also a price check. It's the smaller one, built to ride in a pocket. It sat at $9.98 on the November 8, 9 and 10 checks and moved to $12.98 by November 12. With the two tapes ten cents apart, pick by blade length. Shelves and picture spacing suit the Klein. Rooms and rugs suit the Craftsman.

One handle, a lot of bits

The Craftsman 26-piece ratcheting screwdriver and multibit set, model CMHT68001, was $16.00, the same price on every check since November 7. The page shows a list price of $24.05, so this is a price check too. A ratcheting handle lets you keep turning while your grip stays put, and that's the feature to pay for on flat-pack furniture.

The Klein Tools 11-in-1 screwdriver and nut driver was $15.97 on every check since November 8. It pairs common screw bits with nut driver sizes in one tool. If your projects lean toward hex nuts, like appliance panels or bed frames, the Klein is the better fit. If they lean toward screws, the Craftsman ratchet earns its spot.

The DEWALT 40-piece FlexTorq impact bit set, model DWA2T40IR, was $19.89 on November 23, the same price it has held on every check since November 2. Amazon shows a typical price of $21.57. Our badge needs a wider gap than $1.68, so the card is a price check. The listing says the bits are impact rated for cordless impact drivers and come in a case with a clear lid. It's the pick for someone who already owns an impact driver.

Pick a hammer by weight

The Craftsman 20-ounce fiberglass hammer, model CMHT51399, was $16.98 against a list price of $18.98, a price check. The Estwing 16-ounce rip hammer with a smooth face was $24.98, its price on every check since November 8, and it's the most expensive card here. The Estwing is the lighter head and costs $8.00 more. A heavier head sinks bigger nails in fewer swings. A lighter one is easier to control on picture hooks and trim. Buy the one that matches the nails you actually drive.

For gripping and holding

The Klein Tools FLICKBLADE 2-in-1 utility knife and scraper, model 44306, was $20.98 on every check since November 8. It folds, and the card's advice is simple: lock it closed when you put it away. It's a box cutter that doubles as a scraper, and that second job is why it made the list.

The BLACK+DECKER slip joint pliers 2-pack, model BDHT84001, was $9.99 against a typical price of $16.09, so the badge is on. You get an 8-inch and a 12-inch pair of tongue-and-groove pliers with dipped grips, and the listing describes drop-forged steel with a low-profile head for tight spaces. Amazon.com ships and sells it, and the page labels it a Black Friday Deal. The card says to re-check after November 26, 2026. Two sizes for $9.99 makes it the easiest add-on in this guide.

The IRWIN VISE-GRIP Original 11-inch locking C-clamp, model 11R, was $21.19 on every check since November 8. It holds a glue-up or a wobbly joint while you work. Put pads under the jaws on soft wood, since the clamp can leave marks. One clamp is a good start for small repairs. Bigger glue-ups usually call for a pair.

The lowest price on the page

The Amazon Basics 15-foot indoor extension cord 2-pack was $10.08 against a typical price of $13.81, so the badge is on. That works out to $5.04 a cord. They're black, 16 AWG, rated 13 amps at 125 volts, with 3-prong plugs, and Amazon.com ships and sells them. The price was $10.08 on every check from November 11 through November 23. These are indoor cords, so keep them inside for a lamp or a holiday tree.

Keep the ceiling honest

Tax and shipping still sit on top of these prices. The $24.98 Estwing is the card most likely to cross $25 at checkout, and the $21.19 clamp and $20.98 knife are close behind. The prices in this guide are the November 23 check. Each card updates when the deal desk checks it again, and the price in your cart is the one that counts. This update trims the grid to eleven Amazon listings checked on November 23.
