Thanksgiving hosting under $100

Spend $100 on one hosting job before Thanksgiving. Amazon listings were price-checked on October 19, 2026. The Gourmia air fryer still carries a September 29 Target check, and the towel set still carries a September 28 check.

CheapHub Editorial · Updated October 19, 2026 · Selection framework

About this guide

These cards are published catalog listings with a merchant photo and a locked product page. Each card shows the merchant, the price recorded on its last-checked date, how that price was checked, any restrictions, and that date. A verified-discount badge appears only when the merchant page itself showed a typical or list price above the selling price. Every other card is a price check. The Amazon rows here were checked on October 19, 2026. The Gourmia air fryer was last checked at Target on September 29, and the towel set on September 28.

Thanksgiving is November 26. Spend the $100 on the job that is stuck. If the oven is already promised to the main dish, buy the slow cooker and stop there.

A side with a locking lid

The Crock-Pot 2.5-quart casserole slow cooker was $51.71 on October 19, against a typical price of $54.99, so the badge is on. It is white and blue. The listing says the stoneware is oven-safe, both the stoneware and the lid are dishwasher safe, and the settings are high, low, and warm. The locking lid is for carrying. That is the hosting detail: a 2.5-quart side can leave the house. The size is a casserole, and the product page is where the oven-safe claim lives.

A second heat source

The Gourmia 6 qt digital window air fryer was $59.99 at Target on September 29, against a regular price of $69.99. The badge is on for that reg-versus-sale figure, recorded for store 3991. Local and online prices can differ. This is the black 6-quart, TCIN 89110863, with a window in the basket and 12 one-touch presets. It earns the spot when the oven is already full. Treat $59.99 as the September 29 check, and read Target again at checkout.

The Ninja Blender Pro+ (BL660) was $99.99 on October 19, against a typical price of $119.99. That selling price is the top of this page's ceiling. The listing gives it a 1,100-watt motor, a 72-ounce pitcher, and a max liquid capacity of 64 ounces, plus two to-go cups. Removable parts are top-rack dishwasher safe. Use the 64-ounce liquid figure if the job is a pitcher of drinks. Tax can push $99.99 over $100.

Coffee after the plates

The Keurig K-Express in Island Berry was $69.99, against a typical price of $89.99. The reservoir holds 42 ounces, about four 8-ounce cups. The drip tray comes off for a travel mug up to 7.4 inches tall. It turns off 5 minutes after the last brew. Pods are sold separately.

The Happy Belly French Roast pods, 100 count, were $20.38 against a typical price of $33.02. The listing says the coffee is 100% Arabica, packaging may vary, and the pods fit 1.0 and 2.0 K-Cup brewers. Buy this box if the brewer above is the one in the cart. Other Happy Belly counts are different listings.

The Coffee mate Original creamer singles, 180 count, were $12.99 against a typical price of $15.99. Each packet is 0.375 ounces of powdered Original. A liquid creamer bottle is a different product. The card says to re-check after October 20, 2026, unless Amazon still shows this selling price against the typical price.

Cups, then something cold

The Hefty Party On cups, 16 ounces, 100 count, were $9.97 against a typical price of $12.49. Assorted colors. The recorded price is the one-time purchase. A Subscribe & Save coupon for 10% off was on the Amazon page and is not included in that $9.97.

The Poppi Major Mix 12-pack was $20.30 against a typical price of $23.97. Twelve 12-ounce cans, two each of Shirley Temple, root beer, orange cream, lemon lime, cream soda, and cherry cola. The recorded price is the one-time purchase. Subscribe & Save was lower on the page and is not the price on the card.

A spinner you can put on the table

The OXO Good Grips salad spinner was $32.95. No typical price sat above that, so the badge stays off. It is the large size, about 6.22 quarts, with a one-handed pump, and the listing describes it as a spinner that doubles as a serving bowl. Confirm the large capacity if a compact spinner was what you had in mind. The price moves. The October 19 figure is the comparison, and checkout is the price that counts.

Leftovers, then the guest room

The Rubbermaid Brilliance 9.6-cup containers, 2-count, were $27.14 against a typical price of $28.68. Two airtight plastic containers, sized for leftovers that need more room than a sandwich box. Confirm the piece count on the product page before checkout. The Target glass 8-cup is a different container. Its base is the oven-safe part, and the plastic lid stays out of the oven. That glass container is on the kitchen essentials page, not in this grid.

The Bedsure queen sheet set was $14.39 against a typical price of $16.49. Four pieces, queen, 100% polyester microfiber. The listing says the flat sheet and pillowcases have a ruched hem. Sold by Bedsure and shipped by Amazon. A king bed is a different listing.

The Room Essentials 6-piece Everyday Striped dorm towel set was $20.00 at Target on September 28. No regular-versus-sale comparison, so the badge stays off. The card calls it a budget set for a dorm or a guest bath and says to expect thinner fabric than a premium Threshold towel. Confirm the mix of bath towels, hand towels, and washcloths on the product page.

Keep the ceiling honest

Tax and shipping still sit on top of these prices. The $99.99 blender is the pick that can clear $100 once those are included. A slow cooker and an air fryer together are a second decision. The grid is these twelve photographed listings. A roasting pan is not in the catalog with a locked photo this week, so it stays off the page.
